# FluentCanvas · Microsoft Store 多语言介绍

以下文案对应应用的 24 种界面语言选项。葡萄牙语分别提供葡萄牙和巴西版本，共 25 份。每份可分别复制到 Partner Center 的 **Short description（短介绍）** 和 **Description（详细介绍）** 字段。[Microsoft Store 字段说明](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/publish/publish-your-app/msix/add-and-edit-store-listing-info) · [商店语言代码](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/publish/publish-your-app/msix/app-package-requirements#list-of-supported-languages)

## 简体中文（zh-CN）

**短介绍**

在 Windows 桌面上圈出讲义重点，切换到多页白板，一步步写出解题过程。

**详细介绍**

用 FluentCanvas 讲解一道几何题时，可以从浮动工具栏选取荧光笔，在屏幕上标出已知条件，再用画笔给图形添一条辅助线。需要展开推导时，切换到白板，边讲边加页，让学生跟上每一步。

写错一笔，可以单独选中或擦除，也可以撤销和重做。把板书保存在电脑上，下节课就能重新打开，接着讲解。布置限时练习时可以开启倒计时，讨论答案时可以从班级名单中随机抽取一人分享思路。

界面提供 24 种语言选项，默认跟随 Windows，也可以在设置中自行选择。

## 英语 · English（en-US）

**短介绍**

Annotate slides on your Windows desktop, then work through an example on a multipage whiteboard.

**详细介绍**

With FluentCanvas, you can circle a detail on a slide or highlight a passage in a handout as you teach. Pick a pen or highlighter from the floating toolbar, then switch to the whiteboard when you need room to show each step of a solution.

Select or erase individual strokes, undo a change, or redo it. Save your drawing on your computer and reopen it for the next lesson. For class activities, set a countdown for an exercise or draw a name at random when it’s time to share answers.

FluentCanvas follows your Windows display language by default. You can also choose from 24 language options in Settings.
